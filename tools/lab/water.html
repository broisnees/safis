Water lab

Simulated water in E3: a pool whose ripples are solved exactly in Fourier space (drops ring outward at the speed the dispersion relation gives each wavelength, the walls reflect them, a floating ball pushes the water aside) with caustics on the tiled floor; the open sea at sunset under the physical sky; and a glass of water with a straw that bends at the surface. Render with node tools/check-3d.mjs --lab tools/lab/water.html.
